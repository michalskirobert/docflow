# Bundled document fonts

Additional unmodified TTF binaries and their original SIL Open Font License texts were obtained from @betteroffice/fonts 0.4.0. No package runtime or CDN loader is included.

Upstream projects: liberationfonts/liberation-fonts, googlefonts/carlito, huertatipografica/Caladea and google/fonts (Gelasio, Open Sans, Roboto, Source Sans 3).

These families are displayed under their own names. They are not renamed to Arial, Calibri, Cambria, Times New Roman or Courier New.

Each additional family includes regular, bold, italic and bold italic with Polish characters. The app and PDF embed/use identical local TTF files.

Missing DejaVu Sans and Serif italic faces were added from dejavu-fonts-ttf 2.37.3, unmodified; its original license is LICENSE-DejaVu.txt.
